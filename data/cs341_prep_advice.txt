CS 341 - How to Prepare

[Post] I'm going to take CS 341 next semester and I'm very scared. I've heard 
such bad reviews about this course so I want to be better prepared before the 
class begins. Does anyone have any recommendations on how I could prepare?

[Comment] CubicStorm: This was one of my favorite courses and most of the 
complaints about difficulty are a bit overhyped. The main challenge compared 
to prev courses like 225 is that the MPs are more open where they state "your 
program must do this..." but then don't really tell you how — that is for you 
to figure out. The course textbook is at cs341.cs.illinois.edu/coursebook/index.html 
and OSTEP is also a great reference.

[Comment] CubicStorm: The first one is what 341 actually uses, it's quite short. 
OSTEP is one of the best OS books and has more info than you actually need for 
341 but is a great reference without giving too much away.

[Comment] caterpillarcupcake: The number 1 thing you can do is start the 
assignments as soon as they come out. The MPs are manageable if you start early 
but super overwhelming if you wait until the last couple of days. Go to class 
every time and take good notes. Also, debugging is one of, if not the, most 
important skills you will need in this course. 341 was one of the hardest classes 
I took but also one of my favorites. Good luck!

[Comment] klothegg: In addition to the other comments, make sure you understand 
the MP spec before you implement, and write very modular code.
