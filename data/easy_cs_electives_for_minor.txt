[Post] I'm doing a CS minor and looking for recommendations for easier classes 
to take for electives. The options are 357, 361, 374, and pretty much any 400 
level course.

[Comment] UIUC-CScrub: 361 and 374.

[Comment] n3on_tv (CompE '23): If you're in a scientific major 
(Physics/Astronomy/Atmo), CS 357 is very helpful and applicable. CS 440 (AI) 
is a good survey of AI and not the hardest class.

[Comment] n3on_tv (CompE '23): Yes, I would not recommend taking 357 without 
linear algebra. These are the courses I would suggest: MATH 257 (Linear Algebra 
with Computational Applications), CS 357 (Numerical Methods, great to take right 
after MATH 257), CS 361 (Probability & Stats), CS 418/411/412 (easier tech 
electives).

[Comment] ulucecethrowaway999 (Grad): You definitely don't need linear algebra 
for CS 440 itself. It's really a Python coding based intro for the complete 
layman who isn't necessarily planning on doing research into AI. However, you 
definitely need it for CS 446 or anything more advanced. In general, a strong 
linear algebra background will be essential for any applied math related field 
in ECE/CS.

[Comment] nipirennipi2: Cyber Dystopia CS 464 is really interesting and easy 
to satisfy the 400 level CS minor requirement. No coding involved but be prepared 
to read tons of books about downsides of internet and technology and discuss them 
in class.
